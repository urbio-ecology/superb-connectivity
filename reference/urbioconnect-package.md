# urbioconnect: Urban Habitat Connectivity Analysis

Analyse and visualise habitat connectivity in urban landscapes,
accounting for barriers and buffer distances. Includes Shiny app for
interactive analysis and report generation. Based on methods developed
by Kirk et al (2023)
[doi:10.1016/j.mex.2022.101989](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mex.2022.101989)
.
